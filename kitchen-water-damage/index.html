Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Southlake, TX

Cabinets, countertops, tile and the subfloor underneath all hold water, and we dry the whole kitchen, not just the visible spot.

☎ Call (817) 623-9267

Kitchen water damage restoration in Southlake, TX is the process of removing water from a kitchen and drying the cabinets, flooring, walls and subfloor so they can be kept or rebuilt. The room has more water sources than any other in the house, from sink supply lines to the dishwasher and the refrigerator line.

Kitchens are also built in layers. Cabinet bases sit on the floor, flooring runs underneath them, and plumbing lines pass through the back wall, so a small leak can travel a long way before it reaches an open surface.

If water is spreading now, shut the valve under the sink or the home's main shut-off, and stay out of the standing water if outlets or appliances are nearby.

Where kitchen water hides

Most kitchen leaks begin under the sink. A supply line, a drain trap or a garbage disposal seal drips into the cabinet floor, and particleboard soaks it up. The first sign is often a swollen cabinet bottom or a stain on the inside wall of the cabinet.

Dishwashers and refrigerator lines hide water in a different way. A dishwasher leaks into a closed pocket between cabinets, and a refrigerator line leaks behind a machine that nobody moves for months. Both let water run beneath the cabinets and under the flooring before it reaches the open floor.

Emergency safety steps when your kitchen is flooding

Close the shut-off valve under the sink or at the appliance. If that does not work, use the main water valve for the house. Then take stock of electricity, because kitchens have more outlets and appliances at floor level than most rooms.

If water is near outlets, a range or a dishwasher, stay out of it and switch off the breaker only if the panel is dry and easy to reach. If the smell of gas is present, leave the home and call the gas utility or 911 before calling us. Public Works takes after-hours water emergencies at (817) 748-8082.

Clear countertops of food and fragile items, lift small appliances off the floor and open cabinet doors so air can reach the wet bases. A licensed plumber fixes the leak, and our dispatch is open 24/7 for the water.

How the crew dries cabinets, toe kicks and subfloor

The crew begins by extracting standing water from the floor and checking surfaces with a moisture meter and thermal imaging camera. Cabinets get special treatment because particleboard loses its strength when it stays wet, while solid wood and plywood boxes often survive.

Toe kicks are removed to open the space under the cabinets. We may drill small holes or remove a panel on the back so air can reach the cavity. Air movers blow across the openings, and LGR dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the air.

Tile can usually stay if the subfloor dries. Hardwood and laminate often need a decision after readings, and the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard is a reference for how water-damaged materials are handled. Ask any company whether its crews work to it.

Repairs, remodels and what the city says about permits

The City of Southlake permit guide does not require a permit for cosmetic work. It lists painting, wallpapering, carpeting, cabinets and trim as cosmetic. A remodel of an existing building that involves walls, electrical, plumbing or window replacement is on the permit list.

In practical terms, replacing a few cabinets and repainting is different from opening a wall to reroute plumbing. The guide is not all-inclusive, so call Building Inspections at (817) 748-8236 and check your homeowners association covenants before you start.

If a leaking pipe in the wall must be replaced, that is a plumber's job, and the plumber should confirm whether a permit applies. We do the drying, and a contractor you choose handles the rebuild.

Insurance and paperwork for a kitchen loss

The Texas Department of Insurance says claim tips include taking photos or video before repairs, keeping damaged items until the adjuster has seen them, making temporary repairs to limit further damage and holding permanent repairs until the insurer inspects. A broken fitting is usually sudden and accidental, while a long seep is not.

Check your policy, ask your insurer how it handles water in kitchens, and keep receipts for anything you buy. We cannot promise a claim will be approved.

For general city questions, Southlake customer service is at (817) 748-8051. For a water emergency outside the home, call Public Works at (817) 748-8082.

What changes the cost of a kitchen restoration

Kitchens vary more than almost any room. These factors move the scope, and no honest number is possible before someone checks readings.

  • Water category, which depends on the source.
  • Cabinet material and how much of it got wet.
  • Flooring type and whether it can stay.
  • Whether the wall cavity or the room below is wet.
  • How many days of drying and how much equipment is needed.

Mistakes that turn a small leak into a remodel

A few habits cause most of the second-round damage in kitchens. Skipping them helps keep the job small.

  • Wiping the surface and leaving the cabinet base wet.
  • Closing toe kicks and doors over damp cabinets.
  • Caulking over a wet joint.
  • Running a heater in a sealed kitchen.
  • Replacing flooring before the subfloor reads dry.
