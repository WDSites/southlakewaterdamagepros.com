Contact Southlake Water Damage Pros in Southlake, TX

Call the 24/7 line when water is spreading, or email if your question can wait.

☎ Call (817) 623-9267

The fastest way to reach Southlake Water Damage Pros is by phone. The line is open 24/7, so call at any hour when water is spreading, a ceiling is sagging or you have found mold after a leak. We take requests by phone and email, and there is no online request form on this site.

If it is not an emergency, email works too. Send the address, what you see and a good time to call back, and we will reply as soon as dispatch can.

What to have ready when you call

  • The street address and the nearest cross street, so dispatch can plan the route.
  • Where the water is coming from and whether you have shut it off.
  • How long the area has been wet and which rooms or floors are affected.
  • Whether water has reached outlets, the electrical panel or gas appliances.
  • Your insurer's name, if you plan to file a claim, and photos you have already taken.

If there is any electrical or gas risk, leave and call your utility or 911 before you call us. For an after-hours water or sewer emergency inside Southlake, the city's Public Works line is (817) 748-8082.
