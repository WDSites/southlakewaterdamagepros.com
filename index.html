Water damage restoration in Southlake, TX is the work of stopping the water, pulling it out, drying the structure and rebuilding what could not be saved. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, so a burst supply line at midnight and a roof leak on a quiet Tuesday both start with the same phone call.

We send licensed and insured restoration professionals who work from a written scope. The scope lists the rooms, the materials, the drying equipment and the repairs, and the price is explained before work starts. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and we never promise an arrival time we cannot control. Ask any restoration company, including us, whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for professional water damage restoration.

Southlake sits in Tarrant County along State Highway 114, and dispatch also covers Grapevine, Keller, Colleyville and Roanoke. Every page below was written for a homeowner standing in a wet room who needs the next step, not a sales pitch.

Documentation for your insurance claim

We record moisture readings, photograph the damage and write a scope of work you can hand to your adjuster. The Texas Department of Insurance says most homeowner and renters policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, while gradual leaks and floods are usually treated differently.

On a large loss, such as water running for days in a vacant house, tell your insurer early and keep damaged materials until the adjuster has seen them.

Where we work around Southlake

Southlake Town Square, a mixed-use shopping center at State Highway 114 and Southlake Boulevard, packs several businesses into a small footprint. If water comes through the ceiling of a shared building there, tell the property manager and the tenant below before anyone moves wet materials, and give dispatch the unit and entrance so the crew can find the right door.

State Highway 114 runs along the north side of the city and Southlake Boulevard is the main commercial road. When you call, name the nearest cross street on one of those roads along with ZIP 76092, and the crew can plan the route before they leave.

Southlake Water Utilities handles the city's water and sewer service. For an after-hours water or sewer emergency, the city's Public Works line is (817) 748-8082. If a main line is leaking at the street or the meter, that call comes before ours, because only the utility can shut off the supply outside your property.

Beyond 76092, dispatch covers ZIP 76051 in Grapevine, ZIP 76248 and ZIP 76244 in Keller, ZIP 76034 in Colleyville and ZIP 76262 in Roanoke. Whether your repair needs a permit is a question for the Southlake building inspections office at (817) 748-8236, and the city's flood zone map plus the FEMA Flood Map Service Center show the flood risk for your address.
