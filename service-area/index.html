What changes from town to town

Three things differ from one town to the next. The first is the city office that controls the water supply to your street, which decides who can shut off a failed main. The second is the ZIP code, which shapes how dispatch routes a crew and how we describe coverage. The third is the building or permit office, which answers whether a repair needs a permit.

For Southlake, the building inspections office is at 1400 Main Street and its number is (817) 748-8236. Other towns run their own offices, so ask your city before assuming a permit rule you read about somewhere else applies to you. The city's flood zone map and the FEMA Flood Map Service Center are the right places to check flood risk for any address in these towns.
