Oak Street dining district and commercial water losses

When water is spreading in Roanoke after hours

Shut off the main water valve, stay out of any water near electrical equipment and move valuables to a dry place. A gas smell or sparking outlet means leave first and call the utility or 911.

If water is still spreading at a shop or home

Know whose valve it is

Business tenants should find out whether the shutoff is theirs or the landlord's. Homeowners close the main water valve.

Power off if the panel is dry

Kitchen equipment and dining room outlets are risky around water. Cut power only when you can reach the breaker without wading.

Move stock and files

Lift paper goods, food stock and records onto shelves or tables. Plastic sheeting over a shelf can protect from drips.

Keep people out of dirty water

Staff, customers, children and pets should stay out of water from a drain, a grease line or an overflow.

Document, then call

Take photos of ceilings, floors and equipment before cleanup. Then call our 24/7 line so drying can begin.

Home water problems around Roanoke

Homes see the same ordinary failures as anywhere else. A roof leak runs into the attic and a ceiling collapses, a washer supply valve fails, or an upstairs toilet leaks into the room below. Mold can show up in a closet or bathroom after a leak that was never dried properly.

After extraction, water damage cleanup removes wet material that cannot be saved. When the drying logs show the structure is dry, water damage repair puts the ceiling, wall or floor back together.
