When an upstairs leak reaches the rooms below

In a large two-story house, a leak from an upstairs bathroom or laundry can soak a ceiling, run down a wall cavity and reach flooring below. The first sign is often a stain far from the source.

Tell dispatch where the stain is and what sits above it. Our water damage repair page explains what comes after drying, and our mold remediation page covers what to do if wet materials sat too long.

Planning drying across many rooms

A bigger house has more surfaces and more air to dry. Crews map moisture room by room, set air movers and dehumidifiers where the readings are highest and return to check progress.

We do not say large homes always take longer. We say the plan has to cover every wet room, and the scope is written before work starts.

Contents, furniture and what to move first

Move dry valuables, electronics and papers out of the wet area if you can do it safely. Leave heavy furniture alone if the floor is wet or unstable, and ask the crew how to move it.

Keep damaged items until the adjuster has seen them, and photograph them first.

Safety steps for the first hour

Keep children and pets out of any room where water reaches an outlet. The main valve is worth closing if you can reach it dry. A wet floor is no place to flip a breaker, so wait for professional help.

A gas smell, sparks or a sagging ceiling is a 911 call. A suspected city main problem goes to Southlake Public Works at (817) 748-8082. For everything else, call (817) 623-9267 and our emergency water extraction crews can be dispatched.

Insurance records for a larger loss

The Texas Department of Insurance says sudden and accidental water damage is often covered and gradual leaks usually are not. A larger loss means more items to document, so keep a list with photos.

Ask your insurer how it treats your cause. Photos, moisture readings and a written scope can describe the work, and they do not promise approval. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published water damage restoration standard, so ask any company whether its crews work to it.
