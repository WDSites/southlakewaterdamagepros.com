Winter: freeze and thaw bursts in Colleyville

Freeze-and-thaw winters can burst pipes, and the damage often shows up during the thaw rather than the freeze. A split line in an attic, exterior wall or garage lets go when the ice melts and pressure returns, then runs into the ceilings and insulation below. The February 2021 winter storm tested North Texas plumbing and water systems and is a reminder that one hard freeze can reach many homes at once.

Know where your main water valve is before the cold arrives, because the moment a pipe splits is a bad time to hunt for it. If a ceiling is bowing, do not poke it, since trapped water can drop all at once. Call for emergency water removal so a crew can open the cavity in a controlled way.

Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Colleyville addresses, including the quiet overnight hours when a failed line or a storm leak can run for a while before anyone notices.

Spring: storms, gutters and roof leaks

Spring storms can overload gutters, and water that cannot leave the roof edge backs up under shingles and into the attic. The first sign is often a brown ring on a bedroom ceiling a day or two after the rain. Roof repair is a roofer's job, and we handle the wet insulation, drywall and framing underneath.

Surface water is a separate case. If storm water comes in at a door or garage, that is flooding, and home policies do not cover floods. Only a separate flood policy does. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address and ask your insurer what applies.

Frozen line or ceiling drip?

Catch, do not pierce

Set buckets under drips and leave a bulging ceiling alone. A small hole can release a lot of trapped water.

After a storm, do not touch switches or breakers if the floor near them is wet. Call the utility instead.

Summer and fall: slow leaks that hide

Warm months bring AC condensate lines that clog and water heaters that weep. These leaks are slower and quieter, and gradual leaks or seepage are usually not covered by home policies, including the mold they cause.
