Water Damage Restoration in Timarron, Southlake, TX

Timarron spans 17 neighborhoods across two cities, so the first useful question is which city's utility and building office applies to your street.

☎ Call (817) 623-9267

Water damage restoration in Timarron means stopping the source, removing standing water and drying the structure before drywall, trim and flooring start to break down. Southlake Water Damage Pros takes calls from Timarron homeowners around the clock and sends licensed, insured restoration professionals to the address.

Timarron is a large community with its own owners association. The association's website lists 17 neighborhoods and 1,551 homes in total, and that size shapes the questions we ask when you call.

Timarron sits in two cities, and that matters in an emergency

According to the Timarron Owners Association, 15 of its 17 neighborhoods are in Southlake. Two of them, Cascades and Chadwick Crossing, are physically in Colleyville. Their homes pay Colleyville property taxes but carry Southlake mailing addresses and are zoned for Carroll ISD.

For a water emergency that split decides who you call about a city main or a sewer overflow. Check your water bill to see which city serves you. Southlake Public Works takes after-hours water and sewer emergencies at (817) 748-8082, and Colleyville homes should contact Colleyville Public Works. What we ask when you call from Timarron

Give dispatch your neighborhood name and the nearest entrance, not only the street. A community this size has many entrances, and a crew that arrives at the wrong one loses time you cannot spare.

If your section is gated, say so and tell us how a crew gets in. The association's website says Chadwick Crossing is gated and run by its own sub-association, so a gate code or a call to the gate may be needed before anyone can reach your door.

Where water damage starts inside a Timarron house

The causes are the ordinary ones that show up in any house. An upstairs toilet or shower leak stains the ceiling below. A washer hose or dishwasher line fails behind a machine. A water heater leaks into a closet, an AC condensate line backs up, or a roof leak wets the insulation above a bedroom.

We do not claim any of these is more common in Timarron than elsewhere. What matters is how far the water has traveled. Our water mitigation crews map moisture first, so the wet area is known before anything is opened.

First-hour safety steps for Timarron homeowners

Keep people and pets out of standing water if any outlet, cord or appliance is near it. Shut off the water at the main valve only if you can reach it without stepping into water. Do not touch a breaker panel while standing on a wet floor.

Photograph the damage while it is safe to do so, and move dry belongings out of the wet area. For smell of gas, sparks or a sagging ceiling, leave the house and call 911. For a city main or sewer problem, call the city's Public Works line. Then call our line at (817) 623-9267.

Insurance records and your owners association

The Texas Department of Insurance says most homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, but not gradual leaks, and that floods need a separate policy. Check your policy and ask your insurer what applies to your loss.

Photos, moisture readings and a written scope can help describe what happened. Keep damaged items until the adjuster has seen them. If the water came from something shared or outside your walls, ask your association who is responsible, because the City of Southlake says homeowners and associations maintain their own property.

How extraction and drying work

Crews remove standing water with pumps or extraction wands, then use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find wet walls, floors and ceilings. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers dry the structure, and readings are repeated until the materials reach their dry targets.

Water from a sewer or outside source is treated differently from a clean supply line, and wet materials may need to be removed. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for water damage restoration, so ask any company whether its crews work to it. See structural drying for the full process.

Flood maps for a Timarron address

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. The City of Southlake says its interactive map shows the 100-year and 500-year floodplain layers under the Environmental base layers, and the FEMA Flood Map Service Center holds the official maps.

The city also notes that flooding can happen inside and outside mapped areas and that standard homeowners insurance does not cover floods. Look up your own address before you decide on flood coverage.
