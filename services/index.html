Water Damage and Mold Services in Southlake, TX

Six services, one 24/7 dispatch line. Find the one that matches the water you are looking at.

☎ Call (817) 623-9267

Water damage restoration in Southlake, TX is not one job. A burst pipe, a flooded crawl space, a stained ceiling and a closet with mold each call for a different crew, different equipment and a different order of work. This page sends you to the service that matches what you are looking at.

Dispatch is open 24/7 for all of it. If you are not sure which service fits, call and describe the room, the source of the water and how long it has been wet, and we will point you to the right starting point. We explain scope and price before work starts.

Professional water work follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard and mold work follows S520, so ask any company which one applies to your job. The table below matches the most common situations to a service. Water damage services come first because time matters most there, and mold remediation sits last because it usually follows a moisture problem rather than replacing it.

Which service fits what you are seeing

What you seeServiceWhat it covers
Water is coming in or standing on the floor right nowEmergency water extractionPumps and extraction units remove standing water.
The source is stopped and walls, floors or ceilings are wetWater mitigationMoisture mapping and structural drying start the clock on recovery.
Storm water, creek water, groundwater or sewage came inFlood damage restorationContaminated water needs protection, containment and removal of unsalvageable materials.
The water is out and wet materials need to goWater damage cleanupDebris removal, sanitizing and deodorizing.
Everything is dry and the rooms need rebuildingWater damage repairDrywall, ceilings, flooring, trim and paint.
